# Attendance & Leave Insights (Apr 2025 – Sep 2026)

Metrics: M13 attendance rate = (present days + 0.5 × half days) ÷ scheduled days.
M14 late arrival rate = late arrivals ÷ present days.

## Finding 1: Finance & Accounts has the lowest attendance, Data & AI the highest
- What I saw: average M13 is 90.8% in Finance & Accounts and 95.0% in Data & AI, a gap of about 4 points.
- What it may mean: some teams carry more leave or absence, which may be work pressure, leave habits or a small group.
- What HR could do: talk to the Finance & Accounts manager and check staffing and leave planning around busy periods.

## Finding 2: Attendance is lower than a year ago, and AI flags peaked in mid-2026
- What I saw: every month from Apr to Sep 2026 is lower than the same month in 2025, and May 2026 is the lowest month at 92.9%. May, Jun and Jul 2026 also had the most AI flags (16, 17 and 22).
- What it may mean: attendance is slipping slightly, and the AI model is finding more unusual employee-months at the same time.
- What HR could do: review the flagged employees in those months, with care, before drawing conclusions.

## Finding 3: Weekday makes almost no difference
- What I saw: Monday is 94.3% and Friday is 94.0%, a gap of only 0.3 points.
- What it may mean: there is no real "Monday or Friday problem", so weekday rules would not help.
- What HR could do: focus on departments and months instead of weekdays.

## December check
Attendance does not clearly drop in December. The data has only one December (Dec 2025, 93.7%), and it is not among the three lowest months (May 2026, Jan 2026, Apr 2026). One December cannot show a yearly pattern. To test it properly, I would need at least 3 years of data and a look at how the fake data was generated.

## What I am not sure about
- The data is fake (generated), so patterns may come from the generator and not from real behaviour.
- Only 18 months of data, so seasonality cannot be proven.
- Department averages come from small groups (minimum 5 people), so they swing a lot.
- AI flags belong to employee-months, and every month has some, so "flagged month" here means a month with unusually many flags.
